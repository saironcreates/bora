The Problem
worth solving

Turkey's wholesale market runs on WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, and phone calls. Thousands of wholesalers and buyers transact every day through relationships and trust — but with zero infrastructure. Orders get lost, logistics are fragmented, and there's no single place where a buyer can discover, compare, and order from multiple suppliers at once.

Existing B2B platforms were either too expensive (SAP, Oracle), too generic (Alibaba), or too local and limited. Nobody had designed a marketplace specifically for the Turkish wholesale ecosystem — one that understood how these businesses actually work.

"I manage 200 wholesale orders a week through WhatsApp. I need a real system — but nothing I've tried was built for how I actually work."

KTU Wholesale was conceived as that system: a two-sided marketplace connecting wholesalers and buyers, with integrated logistics tracking, bulk order tooling, and a supplier discovery layer that makes the informal formal — without making it feel corporate.

Challenges

Two sides,
one platform

🤝
Two-Sided Trust
Both buyers and suppliers needed reasons to join before the other side existed. Designed a staged onboarding that lets early suppliers list products immediately, while buyers see curated content — building perceived liquidity before it's real.
🚚
Logistics Integration
Wholesale orders vary wildly in size, weight, and destination. Designed a logistics abstraction layer where multiple carriers compete per order, with real-time tracking surfaced natively in the app — so buyers never leave the platform to check delivery status.
📦
Bulk Order UX
Wholesale buying is fundamentally different from retail — minimum quantities, tiered pricing, negotiation, and lead times. Designed a bulk cart system where pricing updates in real time as quantities change, and suppliers can set MOQs and tiered discounts visually.
01
Verified Supplier Network
Every supplier goes through a KYB verification process before listing. Buyers see ratings, response times, fulfilment history, and category specialisations — turning anonymous wholesale into a trusted, transparent marketplace.

Project Timeline

20 weeks,
concept to handoff

Week 1–4
Market Research & Field Work
24 interviews with wholesalers and buyers in Istanbul, İzmir, and Bursa. Competitive analysis of 11 B2B platforms. Key insight: 89% of orders were placed via WhatsApp — the biggest UX opportunity was simply making ordering not require a conversation.
Week 5–7
Strategy & IA
Defined the two-sided platform model: separate buyer app and supplier panel with a shared data layer. Information architecture for 3 user types (Buyer, Supplier, Logistics Partner). Go-to-market strategy: seed with 20 verified Istanbul wholesalers first.
Week 8–10
Design System & Brand
Built the KTU brand identity — the isometric cube logo representing the three parties (buyer, supplier, logistics). Design system with 90+ Figma components, amber accent palette, and a density system that works for data-heavy supplier screens.
Week 11–16
High-Fidelity Design
Full hi-fi for both apps: 84 buyer screens, 61 supplier screens. The bulk cart required 3 design iterations — tiered pricing interaction was the hardest problem. Logistics integration designed as a native module, not an afterthought.
Week 17–20
Validation & Dev Handoff
4 rounds of usability testing with 6 participants each — suppliers and buyers separately. Task success rate reached 91% in the final round. React Native handoff with motion specs, edge case documentation, and a Storybook-compatible component export.
