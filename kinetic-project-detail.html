01

Fashion film
from scratch

The brief was deceptively simple: make a 60-second film that communicates the brand value of a sustainable fashion label launching its debut collection. No budget for a production crew, no studio, no post-production house — just one director and a weekend in İzmir.

The creative direction drew on tactile materials — raw linen, uncut thread, worn leather — and slow, deliberate motion to counter the fast-fashion visual language the brand was explicitly rejecting. The film needed to feel handmade even as the motion graphics remained rigorously geometric.

"Every cut should feel intentional. This is a brand that believes nothing should be made unless it has a reason to exist — the film had to embody that."

Shot on Sony FX3 at 4K/24fps over two days, edited in Premiere Pro, colour graded in DaVinci Resolve with a custom LUT, and motion graphics composited in After Effects. The final film ran across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and as a retail installation loop.

Challenges

One director,
every role

🎬
No Crew Constraint
Shot entirely solo — director, DOP, and focus puller in one. All camera moves were designed to work with a tripod or monopod, and the shot list was engineered so every scene could be set up and struck within 15 minutes to keep the two-day schedule viable.
🎨
Colour Language
The brand palette was earthy and muted, but the distribution platforms (Instagram, TikTok) compress and cool footage heavily. A custom DaVinci Resolve LUT was built to ensure the warm tones survived algorithmic compression while maintaining the slow, analogue feel.
🔁
Multi-Format Delivery
The same 60-second film needed to work as a 16:9 retail loop, a 9:16 mobile vertical, and a 1:1 Instagram post — three separate edits with identical brand feel but completely reframed compositions. Cut and colour graded three versions in parallel.
01
Storyboard & Shot Design
Every shot was pre-visualised in a hand-drawn storyboard before production. 28 shots across 4 scenes, timed to the music's BPM so every edit point lands on a beat or half-beat. The storyboard doubled as the shot list on the day.
